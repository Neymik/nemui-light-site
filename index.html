Nemui Light

Your Apple Watch sleep, in a Google Sheet.

Nemui Light is a small iPhone app that copies each night's sleep from Apple Health into a spreadsheet you own. It runs automatically, so you don't have to open it.

How it works

  1. Allow Nemui Light to read Sleep in Apple Health.
  2. Connect your Google account and create a spreadsheet from the app.
  3. When your Watch syncs a night to your iPhone, the app wakes in the background and adds the sleep stages as new rows.

What it can access

  • Apple Health: sleep only, read-only. The app never writes to Health.
  • Google: your email address, to show which account is connected, and only the spreadsheets Nemui Light creates. It can't see any other files in your Google Drive.

The app has no servers, accounts, ads or analytics. Your data goes straight from your iPhone to your own Google Sheet. See the Privacy Policy for details.
