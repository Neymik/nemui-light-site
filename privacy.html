Nemui Light

Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

Nemui Light ("the app") copies your sleep data from Apple Health into a Google Sheet that you own. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with that data. In short, your data goes only from your iPhone to your own Google account. The developer never receives it.

Data the app accesses

  • Apple Health sleep data. The app has read-only access to Sleep Analysis samples: the start time, end time, sleep stage (for example Core, Deep, REM or Awake) and sample identifier. It reads up to the last 30 days. It doesn't read any other Health data or write to Apple Health.
  • Your Google account email address (openid and email scopes). The app uses it only to show which account is connected.
  • Spreadsheets created by the app (drive.file scope). The app can create a spreadsheet, list the spreadsheets it created, read the sleep rows in them (to skip rows that are already there and to build a nightly summary), add new rows, and add and rewrite its Sleep Summary tab. It can't see or change any other file in your Google Drive.

How the data is used

The app uses this data only to add your sleep to the spreadsheet you choose and to show the export status in the app. It doesn't use your data for any other purpose.

Where the data is stored

  • On your iPhone: a Google sign-in token in the iOS Keychain, plus your email address, the chosen spreadsheet's name and ID, and a short log of recent exports (times and row counts) in the app's local settings.
  • In your Google Drive: the spreadsheet with your sleep rows. It belongs to your Google account and is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

The developer runs no servers and doesn't collect, store or have access to any of this data. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Sharing

The app sends your data only to Google's APIs, so it can be written to your own spreadsheet. Your data isn't sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI or machine learning models. The app doesn't store Health data in iCloud.

Google API Services

Nemui Light's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices and deleting data

  • Tap Disconnect in the app to delete the Google token, email address and spreadsheet choice from your iPhone.
  • Remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Turn off Sleep access in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Nemui Light.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone. Your spreadsheet stays in your Google Drive until you delete it.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
